### 1. Summary
**Merostatus (Nepal Daily Hisab)** is an offline personal daily income, expense, and savings tracker. We take your privacy seriously: **Merostatus does not collect, transmit, store on servers, or share any personal, financial, or device data.**

---

### 2. Information Collection and Storage
* **100% Offline-First:** All information you enter (including transactions, amounts, categories, dates, and notes) is saved strictly in a local SQLite database on your device.
* **No Account Required:** No registration, login, phone number, or email is needed to use the application.
* **No Cloud Transmission:** The application does not communicate with any external backend servers or cloud services.

---

### 3. Permissions & Network
* **Zero Sensitive Permissions:** The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, location, storage files, or phone state.
* **No Internet Needed:** The app functions entirely offline.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising
* **No Ads:** The app does not contain advertisements or advertising SDKs.
* **No Trackers:** The app does not embed analytics trackers, telemetry libraries, or profiling scripts.

---

### 5. Data Deletion
You can permanently delete all locally stored records at any time using the "Clear All Transactions" option in the Settings tab, or by uninstalling the application from your device.
