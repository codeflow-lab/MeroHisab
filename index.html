Privacy Policy for Merostatus (Nepal Daily Hisab)

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: Merostatus does NOT collect, transmit, store on servers, or share any personal, financial, or device data. All your financial transactions and records are stored exclusively on your device's local memory.

1. Overview

Merostatus (Nepal Daily Hisab) ("the App"), developed by Gita Subedi and published by FlowLabsOfficial, is designed as a private, offline personal finance, income, expense, and daily budget tracker for users in Nepal and worldwide.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The App operates under a strict Zero Data Collection policy:

  • No User Account: You do not need to register, create an account, log in, or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use the App.
  • Local SQLite Storage: All data entered (such as income amounts, expenses, notes, dates, and categories) is stored strictly in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • No Cloud Sync: The App does not transmit or synchronize your financial data with any external cloud server or third-party database.

3. Internet Access and Permissions

Merostatus does not require internet connectivity to function and does not request any sensitive Android device permissions (such as Contacts, Camera, Microphone, or Location).

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

  • No Advertisements: The App contains no third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) or advertising trackers.
  • No Analytics SDKs: The App does not embed any analytics or telemetry tracking SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics or Facebook Pixel).

5. Data Export and Deletion

  • Data Control: You have 100% control over your data at all times.
  • Clear Data: You can permanently erase all stored records at any moment by using the "Clear All Transactions" option in the App Settings.
  • Uninstalling: Deleting or uninstalling the App from your device permanently removes all locally stored records.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not target or knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, children can safely use the tool for basic arithmetic and budgeting calculations.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
